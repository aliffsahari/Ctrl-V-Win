WHAT IT DOES
The AI feature and everything around it.

CLASSES
- AIHelper         (the only class that talks to the AI service)
- AILogger         (appends every shown AI reply to ai_log.txt)
- SpendingAdvisor  (builds the prompt and returns the AI's advice)

WHAT THEY DO
- AIHelper: String ask(String prompt) sends the request with
  HttpURLConnection. The JSON is built and parsed by hand with indexOf and
  substring (no external libraries). It reads API_KEY, API_URL and MODEL
  from config.txt and throws AIServiceException when AI is unavailable.
- AILogger: writes the prompt and reply, with a timestamp, to ai_log.txt.
- SpendingAdvisor: takes an account and its transactions, builds a prompt,
  calls AIHelper, logs the reply and returns it.

WHAT THEY DO NOT DO
- Never crash the program when AI fails. The caller catches the exception
  and shows a fallback message.
- Never contain the real API key.

TALKS TO
model (to read accounts and transactions) and exception (AIServiceException).

REMINDERS
- Every AI request must go through AIHelper.ask().
- Check your provider's documentation for the exact URL, headers and reply
  format, then adjust buildRequestJson() and extractReply().
- Add a short "educational only, not financial advice" line to the output.
- config.txt is in .gitignore. Share config.txt.example instead.
